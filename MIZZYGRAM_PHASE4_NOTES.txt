MIZZYGRAM — PHASE 4 (Explore, Search, Hashtags, Trending)
============================================================

UPDATED FILES (replace the existing ones at the ROOT of the repo):
  mizzygram.js
  mizzygram.css

Nothing else was touched. Same single-device storage as before.

IMPORTANT: the community accounts' seed posts changed slightly (a few
now include hashtags), so this update bumps the seed version and will
quietly re-seed their posts once on first load. Anything Lizzy or
Mikael actually posted is untouched — only the fictional accounts'
starter posts refresh.

WHAT'S NEW

1. Explore, rebuilt.
   Open the Explore tab (the search icon) and you'll now see:
     🔥 Trending   — posts with the most reactions + comments recently,
                     weighted so new activity outranks old activity
     ❤️ Popular    — posts with the most reactions of all time
     Suggested accounts — accounts you don't already follow, with a
                     one-tap Follow button right there
     Recent        — the plain chronological grid, like before

2. Search.
   There's a search box at the top of Explore now. Type anything and
   it searches, live, across:
     Accounts   — by username or display name
     Hashtags   — by tag text, with how many posts use it
     Posts      — by caption text
   Clear the box to go back to the Trending/Popular/Suggested/Recent
   view.

3. Hashtags.
   Any #hashtag typed into a caption is now a real, clickable tag —
   in the feed, in Explore search results, everywhere. Tapping one
   opens a dedicated page (#hashtag/tagname) showing every post that
   uses it. A few example tags are already seeded into the community
   accounts' posts so there's something to click right away:
     #BowlingQueen         (Bowling Federation)
     #MizzyGram            (LizzyOS)
     #MrPerfect             (Mikael HQ, Micky's Daily News)
     #LittleMissAttitude     (Micky's Daily News)
   Add your own any time — just type # + a word in a caption.

4. Trending badges.
   Posts that top a category get a small badge on the photo itself:
     🔥 Trending          — top 3 by recent engagement velocity
     ❤️ Most liked        — top 3 by ❤️ Love reactions specifically
     💬 Most commented    — top 3 by comment count
     😂 Most reacted      — top 3 by total reactions of any kind
   A post only shows one badge (its highest-priority one, in the
   order above) so the photo doesn't get cluttered.
