HOW IT WORKS
  Open Our World -> tap the MizzyGram button.
  Posts, likes, comments and the Follow state are saved in the browser
  (IndexedDB) on the device Lizzy uses. Phase 1 is single-device; it will
  move to the Worker when Mikael/other users are added.

  (username, name, bio, avatar).
