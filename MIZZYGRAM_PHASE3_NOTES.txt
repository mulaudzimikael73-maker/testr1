MIZZYGRAM — PHASE 3 (Build The MizzyGram Community)
=====================================================

UPDATED FILES (replace the existing ones at the ROOT of the repo):
  mizzygram.js
  mizzygram.css

Nothing else was touched. No new asset files needed — every fictional
account's avatar and posts are drawn on the fly as little gradient +
emoji SVG cards, so there's nothing extra to upload. Still
single-device (IndexedDB), same as Phase 1 & 2.

WHAT'S NEW

1. Seven fictional accounts, each with their own look and voice:
     @mikaelhq            🏢  Mikael HQ           — dry command-centre memos
     @lizzyos              💻  LizzyOS             — glitchy, affectionate system logs
     @bowlingfederation     🎳  Bowling Federation  — over-the-top sports commentary
     @chocolateemergency    🍫  Chocolate Emergency — dramatic crisis alerts
     @bankofmicky            💰  Bank of Micky       — mock-formal Token Jar banking
     @mickysdailynews         📰  Micky's Daily News  — tabloid headlines about you two
   Each has a bio, a personality, a handful of seed posts on their own
   profile, and already follows both Lizzy and Mikael (Micky's Daily
   News follows literally everyone — it's the paparazzi account).
   They're all tagged "bot" on their profile so it's clear they're
   fictional, not real accounts.

2. Their posts are real MizzyGram posts.
   Each account's seed captions (e.g. ChocolateEmergency's "🚨
   CHOCOLATE ALERT 🚨") turn into actual posts on their profile and in
   Explore, with a generated announcement-card image, backdated over
   the last several days so the feed doesn't look like it was all
   posted at once.

3. The community reacts to you.
   Whenever Lizzy or Mikael posts a new photo, 2–5 of the fictional
   accounts show up over the next few seconds to react (their favourite
   emoji most of the time — Chocolate Emergency leans 🍫, Bowling
   Federation leans 🎳, etc.), and about 60% of the time one of them
   also leaves a comment from their personality-flavored comment bank
   — occasionally as a reply to an existing comment instead of a new
   one. It's throttled and randomized on purpose so it feels like
   people dropping by, not a bot spam attack. Existing posts that
   never got any love also get a first pass of this the next time the
   app loads.

4. @Mikael, built out separately.
   Mikael already had his own real profile (Phase 2) with the "Switch
   to Mikael" button for when he's actually using the app himself —
   posting, commenting, liking and replying as himself. On top of
   that, he now has a quiet passive presence too: when Lizzy posts and
   Mikael isn't the one currently driving the app, he's included in
   the pool of accounts that may react — so it still feels like he's
   there even when he's not holding the phone.
   He also now has Stories — tap his avatar at the top of the Home
   feed to see a couple of seeded ones. Tap left/right (or use the
   arrow keys) to move between them; they're marked as "seen" per
   person so the ring fades once you've viewed them.

EDIT ACCOUNTS / PERSONALITIES
  Open mizzygram.js and edit the CONFIG.users block near the top —
  add/edit bio, posts, comments (the comment bank used when reacting),
  favReaction (their most-used emoji), and tile (the emoji + two
  gradient colors used to draw their avatar and post cards).

After GitHub Pages deploys, hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R). If you'd
like a completely fresh run of the seeded posts/stories (e.g. after
editing captions), clearing the site's IndexedDB storage for the page
will make it reseed on next load.
