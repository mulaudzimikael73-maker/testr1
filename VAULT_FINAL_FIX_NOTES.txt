VAULT FINAL FIX

The Vault is now a random-prize feature INSIDE Secret Shelf.

Two access paths:
1. Free Vault Token: Daily Reward's lizzyRewardPerksV1.vaultFree is recognized and opens the Vault immediately.
2. Vault bid: bid is sent to Cloudflare/Telegram. Mikael accepts the bid. The client polls for acceptance and opens the Vault, granting one random prize.

No individual Secret Shelf item is claimed by the Free Vault Token.

Random prize pool:
Common: +10 MB, +15 MB, Seed Pack, Second Chance.
Rare: +25 MB, Song Exchange, Voice Note Request, Truth Card.
Epic: Question Call, Bank Heist (30-60 MB).
Legendary: Vault Jackpot (+75 MB), Mikael's Wild Card, The Queen's Cut (+50 MB).

Odds: 45% common / 30% rare / 17% epic / 8% legendary.

All JavaScript files pass node --check.
