What changed
------------
1) Seeds added to the Seed Shop for flowers that already had art but no
   seed: Carnation (4 MB), Daisy (4), Poppy (4), Daffodil (4), Pink Rose (5),
   White Rose (5), Hydrangea (5), Chrysanthemum (5), Hibiscus (5), Peony (8),
   Iris (8). Priced to match the existing Common/Uncommon/Rare bands already
   used by Tulip/Rose/Snapdragon/Lily/etc.

2) 7 new flowers added (art cropped from your reference sheet, seeds in the
   shop too):
     Marigold (Common, 4 MB), Baby's Breath (Common, 4 MB),
     Zinnia (Uncommon, 5 MB), Camellia (Uncommon, 5 MB),
     Protea (Rare, 9 MB), Ranunculus (Rare, 9 MB),
     Blue Rose (Legendary, 24 MB)

3) 5 new trees added (also cropped from your reference sheet):
     Cacao Tree (Epic, 20 MB), Weeping Cherry (Rare, 26 MB),
     Redwood (Epic, 32 MB), Baobab (Epic, 34 MB),
     Wisteria Arch (Legendary, 40 MB)

4) The 6 new non-legendary flowers (Marigold, Baby's Breath, Zinnia,
   Camellia, Protea, Ranunculus) were also added to STANDARD_FLOWERS, so
   they can show up from "random flower" rewards too — same as your other
   standard flowers. Blue Rose was left out of that pool, same as
   Moonflower, since it's Legendary-tier.

Everything else (Garden growth logic, weather, watering, etc.) is
untouched — this is purely new catalog entries plus the matching seed
images.
