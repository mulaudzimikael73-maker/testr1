MIZZYGRAM — PHASE 2 (Make It Social)
=====================================

UPDATED FILES (replace the existing ones at the ROOT of the repo):
  mizzygram.js
  mizzygram.css

Nothing else was touched — mizzygram.html and coop.html/coop.css are
unchanged. No Cloudflare Worker change needed. Still single-device
(IndexedDB), same as Phase 1.

WHAT'S NEW

1. Mikael is now a real second profile.
   Since Followers/Following don't mean anything with only one account,
   Mikael got added as a second user (assets/mikael-appears/mikael-1.jpg).
   Mikael already follows Lizzy by default (a nice touch, not toggleable
   from Lizzy's side since it's his relationship, not hers).

2. Switching who you're "using" MizzyGram as.
   Open the Profile tab -> your own profile now has a
   "Switch to Mikael" / "Switch to Lizzy" button. Whoever is acting
   flips who posts, likes, comments, and follows are attributed to.
   This is the workaround for being single-device until Phase 3 wires
   this up to the shared Worker so you can each use your own phone.

3. Likes -> Reactions
   Tapping the heart still works exactly like before (defaults to ❤️
   Love) and double-tapping a photo always hearts it, Instagram-style.
   Press-and-hold the heart button to pick one of the 8 MizzyGram
   reactions: ❤️ 😂 😈 😍 🔥 🎳 🍫 👀. Tap the reaction count under a
   photo to see who reacted and with what ("who liked it", upgraded).
   Old Phase 1 likes are migrated automatically into ❤️ reactions the
   first time you load the app — nothing is lost.

4. Comments
   Add, reply (one level of threading, indented), like, and delete your
   own comments (a Delete link only shows on comments you wrote).

5. Follow system
   Profile now shows real Followers / Following counts. Tap either
   number to see the list. Visiting someone else's profile (tap their
   avatar or username anywhere — a post, a comment, a reaction list)
   shows a real Follow/Unfollow button.

EDIT USERS / REACTIONS
  Open mizzygram.js and edit the CONFIG block at the top — CONFIG.users
  for either profile's name/bio/avatar, CONFIG.reactions for the emoji
  set.

After GitHub Pages deploys, hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R).
