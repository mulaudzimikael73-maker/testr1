HOW IT WORKS
  MizzyGram data lives in Lizzy's browser, so HQ can't touch it directly. HQ now
  drops commands into a queue on the Worker; Lizzy's MizzyGram checks it every
  ~10 seconds (while open, and while it's "using" MizzyGram as Lizzy), applies each
  command once, and acks it. It also publishes a small snapshot of recent posts +
  comments so HQ can list them.

HQ -> MizzyGram tab
  28. Create MizzyGram Post: photo (optional; a caption card is made if empty), caption,
      hashtags, mood (same list as My Mood), audience (Everyone / Lizzy only 💗).
      "Lizzy only" posts get no community reactions and are badged "just for Lizzy".
  29. Interaction Control (as Mikael): like, send any of the 8 reactions, comment,
      reply to a chosen comment, pin a comment (shows 📌 Pinned, sorted to the top).
  30. Fictional Accounts: the account chips choose who posts — Mikael, @BankOfMicky,
      @BowlingFederation, @ChocolateEmergency, @MickysDailyNews.
  31. Event Control: Viral Moment, Bowling Challenge, Breaking News,
      Mystery Account (the Mystery Viewer event), Mikael Surprise.
  Lizzy gets a notification for HQ posts and pins (Mikael's land under 💗).

LIMITS
  - "Audience" is a badge + no-community-reactions, not true hiding (both of you share one browser store).
  - Commands only land while MizzyGram is open on Lizzy's device.
  - The snapshot upload endpoint is unauthenticated (like annoy_consume); it only holds
    captions/comment text, and reading it requires the HQ key.
