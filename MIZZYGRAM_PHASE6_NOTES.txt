MIZZYGRAM — PHASE 6 (Make It Part Of Your World)
=================================================
UPDATED FILES (replace at the ROOT of the repo): mizzygram.js, mizzygram.css
Nothing else touched. Same single-device storage. Hard refresh after deploy.

1. MIZZYGRAM NEWS
   Home now has a "MizzyGram News" banner (latest headline) -> #news feed of fake
   headlines (seeded + generated from real activity: new posts, trending,
   achievements, events). Tap a headline with a post to open it.
2. RANDOM EVENTS (every ~3+ min while the app is open, 50% chance per check)
   🚨 Breaking News · 🔥 Viral Moment (community piles onto a post) ·
   👀 Mystery Viewer · 💗 Mikael Surprise (he reacts/comments on Lizzy's latest post) ·
   🎳 Bowling Challenge (Bowling Federation posts) · 😂 Comment Bomb (5 comments) ·
   🕵️ Classified Post (blurred until you tap to declassify).
   Each also lands in News + Notifications (new 🚨 Events filter) + a toast.
   The News page has a "Trigger an event" button for testing.
   Tune: CONFIG.eventGapMin in mizzygram.js.
3. ON THIS DAY
   Home + News show posts from 1 week / 1 month / 3 months / same date in earlier years.
   Add your own real memories in CONFIG.memories, e.g.
     {month:9,day:24,year:2025,text:"Something happened between Mikael and Lizzy..."}
4. SOCIAL ACHIEVEMENTS
   #achievements page (Profile -> 🏆 Achievements): 🌱 First Post, ❤️ 10 Likes,
   🔥 Trending, 🚀 Viral, 💬 Conversation Starter, 🎳 Bowling Influencer.
   Unlocked ones also show as emoji under the bio. Edit REWARDS to change them.
