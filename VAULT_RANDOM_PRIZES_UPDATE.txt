Vault is a random-prize feature inside Secret Shelf.

Access:
- Successful Vault bid: Mikael accepts -> Vault opens -> one random prize.
- Free Vault Token: Lizzy opens immediately -> one random prize.
- Vault bid is an offer and is not deducted automatically.

Prize pool:
Common: +10 MB, +15 MB, Seed Pack, Second Chance.
Rare: +25 MB, Song Exchange, Voice Note Request, Truth Card.
Epic: Question Call, Bank Heist (30-60 MB).
Legendary: Vault Jackpot (+75 MB), Mikael's Wild Card, The Queen's Cut (+50 MB).

Odds: Common 45%, Rare 30%, Epic 17%, Legendary 8%.

Every prize routes to its real system:
money -> Bank of Micky
seed -> Garden
Second Chance -> Token Jar rerollCredits
interaction tokens -> Token Jar
