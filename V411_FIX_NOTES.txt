V4.11 fixes the two remaining issues reported after V4.10.

WHY THE ICONS WERE VISIBLE BUT DEAD
The icons are static in index.html, but living-desktop.js can run before those DOM
elements exist. The previous icon helper therefore had nothing to bind to.
V4.11 explicitly re-binds all four apps after DOMContentLoaded and again on load.

WHY CODY'S PHOTO DID NOT APPEAR
The previous theme depended on a wrapper class being added by a broad detector.
V4.11 watches the actual .classifiedReader and applies the photo directly when its
visible text identifies Cody Legal Documents / Cody Aladeen legal counsel.
