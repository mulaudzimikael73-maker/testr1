IMPORTANT TIME RULE
- Lizzy Life never compares against the real-world date/time.
- There is no last-seen timestamp and no offline catch-up.
- Closing the Life Mode window freezes the Life clock.
- Hiding the browser tab freezes the Life clock.
- Coming back days/months later leaves the Life date exactly where it was.
- Normal/Fast only advance fixed simulation ticks while the Life window is visibly open.
- Skip Time/Fast Simulate explicitly moves the virtual clock.
